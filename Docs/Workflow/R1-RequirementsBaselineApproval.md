## Baseline Effect

Formal gate approval establishes these artifacts as binding inputs for R2 Engineering Proof and Mechanical Baseline. Every proposed requirement change follows the controlled change process and carries impact analysis for calculations, simulation, tests, software contracts, and manufacturing outputs.

## Successor Stage

R2 begins after formal approval of this gate. R2 produces load cases, calculations, FEA, joint engineering, representative joint-test definition, and the Mechanical Baseline.
